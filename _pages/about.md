I'm an AI scientist working on AI security and production 3D machine learning. I care about
making deep learning systems robust, interpretable, and safe to deploy, and I like working
at the boundary between research and engineering.

**Research.** My PhD at Boston University focused on deep learning security through
explainability and robustness. I authored [TrojDRL](https://doi.org/10.1109/DAC18072.2020.9218663)
([code](https://github.com/pkiourti/rl_backdoor)), the first backdoor attack on deep
reinforcement learning (DAC 2020), and [MISA](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3485832.3485908),
an online defense against trojaned models (ACSAC 2021), and built backdoor detection for the
IARPA TrojAI program at SRI. In [Rethinking Robustness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06665)
(arXiv 2025), I proposed a new way to evaluate the robustness of attribution methods. My work
has been covered by WIRED and Schneier on Security. Lately I study backdoors and jailbreaks in LLMs.

**Industry.** I'm a Senior AI Scientist at SmileShape, building an ML pipeline that turns 3D
jaw scans into mill-ready denture designs in under 2 minutes, 20× faster than manual design.
Before that, at Carbon3D, I shipped 3D ML that orients denture scans for printing at a 99.7%
success rate, which opened the way to a new revenue stream of over $1M/year.
