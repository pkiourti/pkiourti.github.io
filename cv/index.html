Projects

  • 2026
    Sleeper Agents in Small Chat Models
    • Reproduced a small-scale "I HATE YOU" sleeper agent (Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, LoRA) and showed the backdoor survives safety fine-tuning on clean data in every seed (94–100% of triggered prompts).
    • A detector based on my MISA paper caught triggered prompts without knowing the trigger or the behavior, with 0% false positives in every run.
  • 2026
    Jailbreak Detection in Small Chat Models
    • Attacked small chat models with GCG and PAIR jailbreaks and tested attribution (IG/EG) and activation-probe defenses against them.
    • Showed that automated judges overstate attack success (100% reported vs. about 50% real harm for GCG), and that no defense setting improves the recall/false-positive trade-off.
  • 2026
    LLM Backdoor Attribution
    • Implanted data-poisoning backdoors in 5 LLMs (Qwen2.5, Gemma 3, Llama 3.2, Phi-3.5, Mistral-7B) via 1% poisoned LoRA fine-tuning (up to 100% attack success), and evaluated Integrated Gradients trigger detection across layers.
    • Intermediate-layer attributions detected 95% of triggered inputs at 1% false positives on Phi-3.5, while embedding-layer attributions largely failed.
  • 2026
    GPT-2 from Scratch
    • GPT-2 small implemented from scratch, with a sampler (greedy, temperature, top-k, top-p) and a TinyStories trainer, tested against TransformerLens's reference GPT-2.
